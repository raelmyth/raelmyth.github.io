Gal vault maker

Turns your login and client list into an encrypted vault.js. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Country names must match the globe exactly. Every line needs straight quotes, and a comma after each country except the last.

  1. Copy the text above.
  2. In your GitHub repo, open vault.js, click the pencil icon, delete everything, and paste.
  3. Commit. Wait a minute or two, then log in with the username and password you just chose.
